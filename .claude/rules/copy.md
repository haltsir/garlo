# Copy rules (every user-facing string, comment, doc and commit message)

- No em-dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a colon, parentheses, or a new sentence.
- Verdicts are one sentence, present tense, subject first: "Copy is read-bound", "Wi-Fi is the limit", "Safari is starved by Compressor".
- The cause names the resource, the reason and the evidence in one or two sentences, with numbers that carry units (MB/s, ms, percent, Gb/s).
- Actions are imperative and specific ("Pause Torrent", "Reconnect without the hub"), and say the expected effect where one is known.
- `pending` says what Garlo is waiting for; `tierHint` says what it could not see and what would sharpen the verdict. Neither apologises.
- Numbers reach the user only through `Units`. Percentages are written "80 percent" in prose and "80%" in evidence lines.
- Settings hints are one sentence and describe the effect, not the mechanism.
